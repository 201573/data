RESEARCH IMPLICATIONS:

This analysis provides longitudinal evidence for the mediating role of
cognitive effort in the relationship between childhood trauma and meaning
in life. The findings suggest that:

1. Higher childhood trauma is associated with lower cognitive effort
2. Lower cognitive effort is associated with lower meaning in life
3. Cognitive effort may serve as an intervention target for individuals
   with childhood trauma history
